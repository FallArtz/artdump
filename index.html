Who am I?

Hi there! My name is Kiri, and I'm from New Zealand. Online, I go by the artist name of FallArtz.

I'm a Digital Artist, so my specialty is Procreate. I draw using an iPad and apple pencil. I enjoy experimenting with traditional mediums too! For many cards that I've made for friends, you can find doodles of creatures on the insides of the covers. I've done some painting work for school projects too.

I mainly draw ferals—animals that have preserved their natural state—which are generally four-legged. I draw anything else, too! I have a dislike of drawing humans, but I think I've gotten better recently.

You can find me on Artfight! My first fight was in 2024, and I've been in every fight since. Stardust was my very first team.

Things I like!

I like cats, geese, foxes, the colour yellow, drawing, playing games, reading, playing music, listening to music, and writing. I'm knowledgeable about and in many, many fandoms, but I hate listing things I know; feel free to ask me what I like if you're curious :].

I have a black cat! Her name is Luna. She's such a silly gremlin, all of my clothes are coated in her fur. I'm a musician that plays two instruments! The first instrument I learnt was the Piano, which I started in the middle of 2021. On December 2, 2025, I started learning the Viola too. I've been enjoying it so far!
